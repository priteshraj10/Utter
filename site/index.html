Wispr Flow–Style Floating Bar

A minimal, distraction-free floating pill at rest that expands on hotkey. Includes real-time audio waveforms, live WPM typing metrics, and instant cancel.

📞

System Call & Meeting Recording

Record Zoom, Teams, Meet, and browser calls in Notes. Utter captures both your microphone and computer audio natively using CoreAudio taps without screen recording permissions.

🔒

100% Offline & Zero Cloud Lock-in

Powered by Whisper (Small/Medium/Turbo/Large) and Silero VAD. Accelerated by Metal on Apple Silicon and Vulkan on Windows/Linux. Zero raw audio leaves your computer.

🇮🇳

Mixed Hindi + English (Hinglish)

Speaks your language. Transcribes natural code-switching seamlessly without garbled phonetics or unwanted translation into pure English.

🎯

Context-Aware Tone Adaptation

Utter adapts writing tone according to the active frontmost app: casual for Slack, polished for Mail, structured for Notion, and technical for VS Code & Cursor.

🍏

Apple Intelligence & Local LLMs

Runs natively on Apple Silicon with Apple Intelligence integration. Also supports Ollama, Groq, Anthropic, OpenAI, OpenRouter, and custom local endpoints.

Default Keyboard Shortcuts

Action macOS Windows / Linux Description
Dictate fn or ⌥Space Ctrl+Space Hold to speak, release to type into active app
Command Mode ⌃⌥Space Ctrl+Alt+Space Rewrite last dictation or edit selected text by voice
Voice Notes & Meetings ⌃⌥N Ctrl+Alt+N Record meeting audio directly into smart Notes
AI Post-Process ⌥⇧Space Ctrl+Shift+Space Dictate with custom AI cleanup prompt
Paste Last Transcript ⌃⌘V Ctrl+Win+V Re-paste the most recent dictation
Copy Last Transcript ⌃⌘C Ctrl+Win+C Copy the most recent dictation to clipboard

All hotkeys can be changed anytime in Settings (Push-to-Talk, Tap-to-Toggle, or Hybrid).
